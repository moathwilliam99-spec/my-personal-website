Featured Project: BotShield

Securing the Digital Frontier Through Advanced AI Bot Detection

Executive Summary

BotShield is a biometric-based security layer designed to distinguish human behavior from autonomous AI agents across high-stakes environments such as online academic testing and high-demand retail platforms.

The Problem

Traditional security measures struggle against sophisticated AI-assisted cheating and automated scripts. Over 51% of global web traffic is now automated, threatening academic integrity and causing retail inventory scalping.

The Solution

BotShield leverages high-frequency behavioral telemetry, analyzing typing cadence, mouse acceleration curves, and micro-variations in user rhythm that automated agents cannot replicate.

Infrastructure Stack

Powered by a scalable cloud architecture hosted on Render and Vercel, secured via MongoDB Atlas database management, and integrated with Stripe for subscription processing.
